About

My name is Emma Healy, and I'm a Senior Product Designer based in the New York metro. I love to work on lean, integrated product teams to streamline existing features or create new flows from scratch. I'm also a certified Section 508 expert, experienced in QA testing and training teams to create accessible products. For the past two years, I have been designing improvements to a large federal agency's case management system on an embedded contract at Alpha Omega.

Before Alpha Omega, I worked as a consultant at Publicis Sapient. My clients included Walmart, Invesco, the Federal Home Loan Bank of New York, and a federal agency. I pitched for new work in the travel and hospitality, financial, and apparel industries. I graduated from Northwestern University in 2021 with a degree in Communications, Design, and Marketing.

I'm always taking classes, whether oil painting at the Decorus Atelier in Brooklyn, watercolor at the New York Academy of Art downtown, or creative writing at the Writers Studio in West Village. I'm originally from a town called Oconomowoc, Wisconsin, and while I make the most of NYC's incredible food scene, I miss fried cheese curds on the daily.
